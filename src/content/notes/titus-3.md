Bishops & elders should teach the congregation to be subject to governing
authorities as long as they aren't against God's law. This is the extent of
"politics" allowed to preach from the pulpit.

### Baptism Saves

We were all vessels of wrath at one point but Christ came to save us by His
righteousness and not our works. This salvation is delivered unto us through
baptism (washing of regeneration) & renewal by the Holy Spirit through Jesus
Christ. This isn't our work, profession, telling the world, etc. This is God
delivering us His death & resurrection unto us in baptism.

A regenerated person should be intent on doing works & living honorably.

### Dealing with heretics

Warn the heretics/divisive members twice then reject him because he continues
to be in sin & has condemed himself. Remember, these are the instructions for
your bishop/elder not for laymen. Do not reject your brothers in error, make
every effort to reconcile them with your good works.
