We have to correct our brothers & sisters when they sin and be cautious that we ourselves do not fall into it. This is how we bear their burden. Test ourselves so that we do not fall into pride 

v6 - very important to tell your teacher/pastor/priest the good things happening in our life. Most of the time we do not talk to them unless we are in trouble

Sin leads to destruction i.e more sin we sow the more danger of falling away from faith. Repentance leads to eternal life. 

## BIG LETTERS TIME
Keeping the law won't save you and those who are compelling us to do that themselves aren't keeping the law either.  It is not the keeping the law, it is the new obedience that we should follow.

v17 - Paul isn't talking about stigmata here. He was literally beaten & tortured for the sake of the gospel.
