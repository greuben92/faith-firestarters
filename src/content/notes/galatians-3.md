Law can never justify us before God even tho it has the power to do so. The problem isn't with the Law of God but rather in us because we can't keep *all* the law. As James notes, breaking any law (moral, civil or otherwise) then we are guilty of breaking the whole law.  So then, what use does the law serve if we know we can't keep it. It's twofold
1. To show us our sin and guide us when our hearts deceive us
2. A custodian and shadow of faith in Jesus Christ

The promises given through law and even 430 years before the law came into existence, it always has been about faith in the Seed. This faith was revealed as early as our first parents fell into the trap of serpent. One's lineage, social status or gender has no bearing on receiving this promise. We receive it through faith alone and as long as we continue in it.

May God grant us bountiful grace that we continue in faith and look towards the crucified & risen One forever. Amen 🙏
