This is a pastoral epistle so we must interpret this book through that lense.

### Introduction

God established the promise of eternal life **according to faith that conforms
to godliness** before time began and by extension the fall of Adam & Eve.
Eternal life has always been from faith &amp; never through works of the Law.

Paul establishes Titus' authority in preaching & ministering by calling him his
true child in the common faith.

### Qualifications for elders

The qualifications are same for every city &amp; throughout time. The mention
of every city disqualifies the modern teaching that pastoral qualifications
were based on cultural contexts as cultures differ largely from city to city.

1. Must be a man, if married then must be a husband of one wife
1. Must be generally blameless in society &amp; not greedy
1. Stick to what has been taught to correct/expose those whose oppose the truth
1. If a father, his children must be in the faith

Most churches would defrock pastors/bishops if they fail any of the first 3
requirements. I'm unsure why they don't follow up on 4th requirement.

Question: should a repentant elder be restored to his position? If yes, then
how does one repant of the 4th requirement.

### Correcting rebellious people

Correct the bad actors using very harsh words otherwise the rest of the
congregation would be in danger too. Jewish myths have already started to
distort the gospel by the time this epistle was written, around 66 AD. The
current jewish myth we have to fight in the present age is dispensationalism
and the many variations of it. Note, this is a pastoral epistle, not for
regular congregation hence harsh words when correcting people should be from a
seat of authority not your online discord server so be civil with fellow
believers and guide them with love.

Finally, we will know true believers by their actions not their words.
