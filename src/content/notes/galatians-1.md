## Historical understanding
Paul is writing against 3 different groups in this chapter
1. Gnostics - who denied Jesus died & taken up in spirit. Hence the reason he starts with Christ died & raised up in v1
2. Judaizers - people who are forcing belivers into following the OT laws. He asserts his past zeal & attests his history of presecution of the church to let them know that he knows the law better than everyone else.
3. False gospels - this one he doesn't address as much as the distorted views of the above 2

## Theological concepts
1. v4 Vicarious satisfation - He gave himself for **our sins**
2. v15 The call to preach is foreordained - Paul being set apart from the womb & the plan revelaed in time
3. v17 Against petrine primacy - He didn't need to consult peter or any other apostles to start his ministry
4. v8 We already know who these people are but the angel part isn't stressed enough cause we still see some angel vision testimonies that are absolutely not found in scriptures. Do not trust even angelic visions if they contradict the already revelaed scripture.
