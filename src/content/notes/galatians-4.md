Paul equates slaves to young sons without inheritance. This should reveal to us the understanding of how the Law expected ancient Israel to treat salves. Through Jesus Christ, we are adopted as sons therefore having all the inheritance promises given in OT.

Paul's sickness is the reason he was able to preach gospel to them. God can use us even in out times of weakness. And, when people receive children of God with gladness, they are receiving Christ himself (v14)

Paul continues crashing out because he knew their zeal but the zeal was being alienated from the truth. 

Finally, Sarah & Hagar are illustrated as two covenants and the chapter ends with "yeet the old covenant & those who follow it" (praphrased) for we have a better covenant
