We are free from law in Christ (v1) but we are warned not to use that freedom for sinful desires (v13). This freedom allows us to freely fulfill the law without any of its condemnations and it is through love we can fulfill it.

Warning against yeast ruining the whole batch i.e people who try to diminish the gospel and they will pay a penalty. And yes, Paul meant what he said in verse 12 and it is exactly what you think it means.

The battle between flesh & spirit will be an ongoing struggle on this side of eternity. Walking by spirit is talking about walking in our new nature after we receive faith. It's not a spiritualised state like gnostics & many pagans still teach. There is a big warning on the dangers of continuing to walk in sin. Then chapter ends with how we are to recognize walking in spirit, it is not by the works of law but by the fruits of the Spirit against which there is no law.
