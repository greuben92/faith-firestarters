**1-5** Personal convictions matters to an extent even in controversies. Judaizers were demanding circumcision of gentiles. After discussing with elders of the church, Paul says, *Titus was not compelled*. He did not force anything but let Gospel & Spirit led conviction run its course. Gospel must be kept pure, not just for us but so that it may continue for future generations.

**6-10** Paul diregards the importance/status/primacy of other apostles & elders as God doesn't show partiality. They had nothing to add to the gospel. Interestingly, the commission to teach jews is entrusted to Peter even though Paul was more knowledgeable in the law. 

**11-14** Peter falls short in matters of faith. Not only was he falling short but he made Barnabas & other jews fall short. Paul condemns Peter privately first and later publicly because they were still acting with hypocrisy

**15-21** Justification will never come to us by keeping the law/works. It is only by faith. V21 makes it perfectly clear if we claim righteousness is from law then we diminish Christ's death to nothing.

### Theological concepts
1. By which means should we discern
2. Infallibility of apostle Peter
3. Righteousness by faith
